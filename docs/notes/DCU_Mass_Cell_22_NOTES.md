## Effective-frequency mapping and error bound

For s=sin^2(theta12), d=d21, phi=K*dee*y and a=K*d*y,

    d31=dee+s*d,
    d32=dee-(1-s)*d.

The weighted fast oscillation is

    (1-s)*sin^2(phi+s*a) + s*sin^2(phi-(1-s)*a).

The weighted first-order Taylor offsets vanish. Since the magnitude of the second derivative of sin^2 is at most two, subtracting sin^2(phi) yields an absolute difference at most `s*(1-s)*a^2`. Multiplying by A gives

    |P_exact - P_onefast| <= A*s*(1-s)*(K*d*y)^2.

This is an analytic uniform bound, not an empirical detector calibration. Over the declared source domain y<=1.2, the bound is .000231699525 in probability; the largest sampled actual difference is .000162269388. Both include the solar term unchanged. Thus the effective-frequency description is useful here but is not the exact three-frequency law or a model of the long-baseline revival.

## Identifiability and precision

At fixed source s,d21, a one-unit change in R changes dee by exactly d21=7.5305380624e-5 eV^2. This is 1.25509 times the Daya Bay nGd quoted .000060 frequency error. It is resolvable as a difference in the likelihood, unlike the nearly invisible R dependence in the earlier KamLAND fold, but not large enough by itself to single out exactly33.

Conversely, with source dee held fixed, R32 can be compensated by a +3.058548% shift in d21, and R34 by a -2.882238% shift. The remaining maximum short-baseline probability changes are about .000657515 and .000601780 over the audited domain, versus .00917662 and .00947536 when the solar scale is held fixed. These alternatives are diagnostic probes of parameter degeneracy, not adopted predictions.

A notional three-error-unit separation of adjacent integer predictions would require sigma(dee)<d21/3=2.51017935e-5 eV^2, approximately .9893% relative to source dee, **if solar inputs are exact and other errors are already correctly represented**. This is not an experimental forecast or a substitute for solar uncertainty and correlations. A true ratio test needs complementary solar and atmospheric information with shared inputs/priors accounted for.
