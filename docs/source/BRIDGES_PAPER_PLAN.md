# From Registry Structure to Physical Observables
## Horizontal Transfers and Operational Bridges in the Distinction Combinatorial Universe

Status: paper plan and bounded completion programme. The new experiments below are proposed, not executed. Existing numerical summaries refer to the saved session results and their named benchmark versions. This document does not alter or stop the separate Mac neutron campaign.

## 1. Paper question and scope

Following *Structure, Symmetry, and Motif Embedding of the 137-Object Distinction Registry*, ask:

> Which physical or operational observables can be calculated from the existing registry-based dictionary and native DCU processes under explicit, transferable correspondences, and where are additional mappings indispensable?

The predecessor characterizes registry structure and motif embedding; it does not identify particles, matter/vacuum, or mass. The new paper tests consequences of stated physical dictionaries and operational attachments. Do not re-prove the registry as though it were a new result, or present the physical assignments as forced by enumeration alone.

Keep two routes separate throughout:

- Horizontal: registry-based parameter prescriptions -> borrowed physical law -> observable -> a specified empirical comparison, or a labeled forward prediction when no direct comparison was made.
- Vertical: native structure/service history -> explicitly declared measurement attachment -> measurable model output -> a held-out native condition or another preparation/readout.

These are complementary routes, not an already closed proof that native motifs possess the dictionary's particle masses. Many vertical results use ancestry sharing and exchangeable service, not specifically the S/I/G counts. Mark registry-specific dependence rather than suggesting all quantum results prove the necessity of 137 or the partition.

Use 'measured' precisely: sampled native counters, simulated Born outcomes, computed probability/density matrices, and laboratory measurements are different evidence types. Current vertical results are model-level calculations, not laboratory validation of DCU or a unique conversion to SI.

### H-new-1 — Pointlike electromagnetic production rate and angular distribution

Question: after threshold kinematics, how does the frozen muon mass transfer into a rate and an angular distribution under an explicitly borrowed QED amplitude?

Process: e+e- -> mu+mu-. Use the screened/structural/measured-mass branches unchanged, the already-declared measured low-energy alpha, and standard hbar,c unit conversion. Pointlike unpolarized one-photon Born formula, neglecting incident electron mass, from PDG 2025 *Cross-Section Formulae for Specific Processes*, section 51.2:

    d sigma/d Omega = alpha^2 beta/(4s) * [1+cos(theta)^2+(1-beta^2)sin(theta)^2]
    beta = sqrt(1-4m_mu^2/s)
    sigma = 4*pi*alpha^2/(3s) * beta*(3-beta^2)/2

Use c=hbar=1 in the formulas and explicit conversion for cross-section units. Keep phase space, charge, spin, amplitude and angular measure labeled as imported physics, not an emergent native scattering process. No new alpha assignment is fitted.

Start with a fixed finite energy panel chosen before output evaluation, e.g. W=0.25,0.50,1.00 GeV, and a fixed cosine grid. Keep total, normalized shape, and mass sensitivity separate; they are not independent tests. Check angular integration, threshold and ultrarelativistic limits. A published empirical comparison is added only if its event/radiation/acceptance definition can be matched with declared inputs. Otherwise stop with a forward cross-section result and a precise boundary, not a fabricated measured success or a new detector reconstruction campaign. Do not ignore running-coupling/radiative corrections when comparing to precise data.

Outputs: one compact numeric table, one response plot if useful, no new Monte Carlo universe. This closes the kinematics-to-amplitude category.

If finite-shot sensitivity is poor, quantify that and stop rather than changing the phase increment or adding a fitted readout.

Conditional information screening is not a causal or gravitational field claim. This is a simple information-theoretic category using the existing record algebra, not a new dynamical mechanism.
